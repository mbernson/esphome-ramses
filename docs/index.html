ESP32-C6 • CC1101 • RAMSES II 868.3 MHz

ESPHome RAMSES Web Flasher

Flash the auto-discovery engine directly from your browser. No command line, compiler, or ESPHome dashboard required.

Your current browser does not support the Web Serial API. Please use Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Opera on desktop to flash the device.
Requires Web Serial (Chrome, Edge, or Opera)

Flashing takes approximately 1 minute. Afterwards, you will be prompted to connect the board to your home Wi-Fi.

Prefer manual flashing? Download raw firmware.factory.bin

How It Works in 4 Simple Steps

1

Connect USB

Plug your ESP32-C6 Sub-1GHz board into your computer with a standard USB data cable.

2

1-Click Flash

Click Install Discovery Engine above, choose your serial port, and wait for the flash to finish.

3

Configure Wi-Fi

The browser will immediately prompt you with an Improv Wi-Fi dialog to select your Wi-Fi network and enter the password.

4

View & Save

Open http://<esp-ip>/ramses to see your heating zones, TRVs, and MVHR units. Click Save to NVS to keep them active forever!

📡

Supported Hardware

This web installer flashes firmware pre-configured for the Elecram / IMMRMKW ESP32-C6 Sub-1GHz Board (or any ESP32-C6 DevKitC-1 with CC1101 on CS=18, SCK=6, MOSI=7, MISO=2, GDO0=15, GDO2=14). For custom pinouts, see the YAML configurations in the repository.
